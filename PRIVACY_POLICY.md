# Privacy Policy for Meal Muse

**Effective Date:** November 13, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 13, 2026  

Meal Muse ("we", "our", or "us") provides the Meal Muse mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our App.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

- **Account & Usage Information:** Meal Muse is designed to assist you with finding and planning recipes. If you interact with our recipe recommendations, meal planner, or favorites, your preferences may be saved locally on your device or synchronized with our secure API (`https://www.mealmuseapp.online/api`).
- **Device & Network Information:** Like most networked applications, basic device information (operating system version, network connectivity status) may be used to deliver network requests and ensure smooth offline/online transitions.
- **Log Data:** We may collect non-identifiable technical logs to diagnose application crashes and performance issues.

---

### 2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:
- Provide recipe recommendations tailored to local ingredients and preferences.
- Allow users to organize and schedule daily meals (breakfast, lunch, supper).
- Maintain, diagnose, and optimize the reliability and performance of the application.

---

### 3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Data is only communicated with our secure backend API to deliver core application functionality.

---

### 4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including HTTPS encryption in transit, to protect data transmitted between your device and our servers.

---

### 5. Children’s Privacy

Meal Muse is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

---

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
